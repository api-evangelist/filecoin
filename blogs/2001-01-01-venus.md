Venus State reliable Theory Audit n/a Edit this section section-implementations.venus Venus, previously called go-filecoin, is another implementation of Filecoin in Go and is maintained by the IPFS Force Community. The go-filecoin implementation, before it was renamed to Venus and taken over by IPFS Force, was already nearly feature-complete (as of June 2020) with go-filecoin nodes interoperating with Lotus nodes. Protocol Labs is offering DevGrants to support the development of Venus.
