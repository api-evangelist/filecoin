Storage Market Actor State reliable Theory Audit done Edit this section section-systems.filecoin_markets.onchain_storage_market.storage_market_actor The StorageMarketActor is responsible for processing and managing on-chain deals. This is also the entry point of all storage deals and data into the system. It maintains a mapping of StorageDealID to StorageDeal and keeps track of locked balances of StorageClient and StorageProvider.
