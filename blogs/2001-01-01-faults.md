Faults State reliable Theory Audit wip Edit this section section-systems.filecoin_markets.onchain_storage_market.faults There are two main categories of faults in the Filecoin network: Storage or Sector Faults that relate with the failure to store files agreed in a deal previously due to a hardware error or malicious behaviour, and Consensus Faults that relate to a miner trying deviate from the protocol in order to gain more power than their storage deserves. Please refer to the corresponding sections for more details.
