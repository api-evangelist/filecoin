Multisig Wallet & Actor State reliable Theory Audit done Edit this section section-systems.filecoin_token.multisig The Multisig actor is a single actor representing a group of Signers. Signers may be external users, other Multisigs, or even the Multisig itself. There should be a maximum of 256 signers in a multisig wallet.
