Miner Collaterals State reliable Theory Audit n/a Edit this section section-systems.filecoin_mining.miner_collaterals Most permissionless blockchain networks require upfront investment in resources in order to participate in the consensus. The more power an entity has on the network, the greater the share of total resources it needs to own, both in terms of physical resources and/or staked tokens (collateral). Filecoin must achieve security via the dedication of resources.
